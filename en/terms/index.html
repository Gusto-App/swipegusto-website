2. What Gusto does

Gusto is an assistant for weekly diet planning. In short:

  • It generates personalized weekly meal plans based on tastes, goals, anthropometrics, cultural preferences, and context.
  • It tracks meals eaten, skipped, substituted, or off-plan ("sgarro").
  • It allows you to take body photos to estimate body composition (body-fat / lean-mass %) and fridge photos for suggestions based on available ingredients.
  • It integrates with Apple Health (HealthKit) for weight, calories, and hydration.
  • It offers a PRO subscription mode (gusto.pro.monthly €5.99/month with a 7-day free trial) unlocking unlimited AI generation, periodic body re-estimation, removal of advertising, and other advanced features.

3. Gusto is NOT a medical device

⚠️ Important. Gusto is an informational and lifestyle-support tool. It is NOT a medical device under EU Regulation 2017/745 (MDR) nor a healthcare service. It does not replace the opinion of a physician, dietitian, nutritionist, or other qualified professional.

Caloric, body-composition, and macronutrient estimates, dish recommendations, and filters tied to health conditions (e.g. diabetes, hypertension, hypercholesterolemia) are based on heuristics, statistical models, and AI models that may produce inaccurate results or results unsuitable for your specific situation.

Always consult a healthcare professional before:

  • Starting a significantly hypocaloric or hypercaloric diet
  • Changing diet in the presence of pathologies (diabetes, hypertension, dyslipidemia, kidney disease, pregnancy, breastfeeding, eating disorders, severe allergies, etc.)
  • Making clinical decisions based on information drawn from the app

In a medical emergency, contact your local emergency number (112 in the EU, 911 in the US) or the nearest emergency room. Gusto is NOT an emergency service.
